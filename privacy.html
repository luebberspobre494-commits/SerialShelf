Privacy Policy

SerialShelf for iOS - last updated 2026-11-04

SerialShelf stores every record on your device. The app has no account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no backend server.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Item records you enter: name, brand, serial number, category, purchase date, warranty length, value and notes.
  • Receipt photos you attach, stored inside the app container.
  • Local notification schedules you create.

Camera and photos

When you scan a label or attach a receipt, the selected image is processed on device using Apple's Vision framework. Text recognition runs locally. Images are never uploaded.

Notifications

Warranty reminders are scheduled locally with the system notification framework. They are not sent from a server.

Exports

CSV export is created on device and handed to the share sheet you choose. We never see it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
