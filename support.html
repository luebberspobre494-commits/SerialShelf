Common questions

  • Where is my data stored? Only on this device. Deleting the app deletes the data, so export a CSV first.
  • How do I move to a new phone? Export a CSV from Settings, then import it on the new device.
  • Why no reminders? Allow notifications in Settings, then reopen the app.
  • Scanning does not find the serial. Photograph the label straight on, fill the frame, avoid glare.
